# Importance of reproducibility in social research
## Practical 4-Task

**Reproducibility** in social research is a vital component for
reliability and validation of research findings.
It not only ensures that research conducted is more credible, but it
also facilitates innovation by enabling other research to build on such
findings. 

**Reproducibility** means being able to consistently replicate findings
under same conditions which strengthens their credibility & ellucidates
that the results were not due to chance. 

*Finally*, if reproducibility is not ensured, this reduces credibility
of findings and can thus result in the reduction of funding as well as
lack of scientific trust. Furthermore, it can lead to wasted resources
by hindering scientific progress as others may base their results on
research that lacks reproducible integrity.
